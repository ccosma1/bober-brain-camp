# Bober Brain Camp mark

Metaphor: **a chunky wooden 3×3 camp slate with one missing square**,
two beaver **ear-bumps** peeking from behind the top rail (ears only —
no square chocolate-bar head, no buckteeth portrait). Puzzle camp:
the last cell is the answer blank. At 32px the read is **grid + hole**,
not a face, not logs, not a sword, not a hut, not a crown, not a moss door.

Shape: **full-bleed square field** of warm camp dusk / ember-brown
`#4A2818`. Primary silhouette is an **axis-aligned timber tablet**
(raised frame, nine square wells). Eight wells are dark wood; **one
bottom-right well is punched cream**. A **gold tack** nails the
top-left frame joint. Two small **ear nubs** sit behind the top rail.
Not a squircle badge, not an inner glow-circle, not a haystack, not a
crown, not stacked capsules.

Palette (4):
- Ember dusk `#4A2818` (field — NOT Crown summer sky, NOT sibling night purple)
- Slate / ear fur `#8B5A2B`
- Cream missing-cell `#F4E6C3`
- Gold tack `#F5C400`
- (supporting) well wood `#5C3A1A`, ink `#1A1028`,
  lit pine board (cream/fur mix so the wells pit — not a dark chocolate bar)

Forbidden (do not reuse):
- **Bober Yeet**: square chocolate-bar beaver FACE as the whole mark, yellow sling-V, giant buckteeth on a square head
- **Bober Dam Defense**: stacked horizontal capsule LOGS, crescent moon
- **Bober Frost Lodge**: snow-capped stick-lodge mound + hearth/chimney flame, hut doorway
- **Bober Nightfall**: wooden stick-sword in ice shard
- **Green Home Games hub**: moss haystack mound + cream door slit
- **Green Home Crown**: 3-point gold crown on grass oval, summer sky field `#87C6E8`
- **Orion / grok-crypto-trader**: constellation, stars, deep-space navy, cyan rim, nebula, rounded-square + glow-circle
- **Lowlight**: iron lantern
- **ViaPass**: cream ticket
- CMC / MultiversX Bober coin art — do not paste or trace
- Text on the mark, wallet/crypto glyphs, clinical brain / MRI / lightbulb-in-head, Mensa chrome
- Night purple `#3A2A6A` as the whole field; Crown sky `#87C6E8` as the whole field

Hard rules:
- Original polygons only. No tracing coin art or sibling icons.
- Full-bleed square, not a squircle badge with an inner glow circle.
- Chunky outline, readable at 16px and 32px.
- Filename `bober-brain-camp.ico` so Windows icon cache cannot keep an old picture.
