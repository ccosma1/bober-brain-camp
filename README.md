# Bober Brain Camp

Fan game by a holder. Not affiliated with any token, studio, or official Bober project.

**Play online:** https://ccosma1.github.io/bober-brain-camp/

A phone-first camp of short puzzle rounds. Phase 1 is **Reasoning + Memory + Speed Assess**, a three-bar **Profile**, and a light **Museum**.

**Honesty:** Fan game skill index — not a clinical or diagnostic test. Not an IQ score. Results are skill bars (0–100) and beta cohort percentiles only. Bars are never averaged into one number. No clinical score, no official test.

Mission: Train at Brain Camp — puzzles, people sense, trivia. Not an official IQ score.

Scope: Short rounds. Play to improve, not to diagnose.

No wallet. No gacha. No PvP. No crypto connect.

## Local

Run `START.bat` or open `index.html` in a browser.

## How to play

1. **Splash** — art preloads (Bober stays visible). Pick **Assess Reasoning**, **Assess Memory**, or **Assess Speed**. **Train** opens drills + trivia (no separate splash Trivia button). **Social Read** is lodge-scenario practice (not an EQ score). **Museum** is open. **About scoring** opens the honesty popup. A camp in progress offers **Continue** / **Abandon**.
2. Each Assess starts with a short **Practice** (unscored), then **Start Assess**.
3. **Reasoning** — 3 practice + 12 matrix items from one of six camp forms (A–F). Eight original families (Row March, Trio Deal, Snow Fade, Stack Add, Dam XOR, Rotate Lodge, Mirror Creek, Beaver Orbit) on stick/dam/snow/beaver/fish/ember/post/tool tiles — not a clinical plate. 50s each. Forms rotate so the same form is skipped for 7 days when possible. Leaving the tab mid-item closes that item as missed.
4. **Memory** — 2 warmups + 10 trials from five glyph packs (lodge tools, snow marks, dam posts, fish, embers). Study 1.8s per icon, 12s to tap. Starts at span 3; span rises after 3 correct at that span, drops after 2 wrong. Span-only: watch the order, then tap the same order. A span-length banner (1.0s) shows before study. Reverse, when it appears, is labeled on the SPAN banner. No tap-what-changed. Blur during the response closes the item.
5. **Speed** — 5 warmups + 20 short trials. Eight templates (odd shape/color/count/tilt/pattern/mark, match-target, twins). 3.2s each. Miss / timeout / leave-tab = missed.
6. **Profile** — Reasoning · Memory · Speed Assess bars, plus **Social Read** practice (lodge scenarios). Never one combined number. Compact **About this skill** notes sit collapsed under each bar. Optional facet tags describe that Reasoning run’s mix only. Honesty depth lives in **About scoring** and **Museum**, not as a wall of text on Profile, so the lodge dinner art can breathe. Social Read does not change Assess.
7. **Museum** — tap a card for detail (large art if unlocked, silhouette tease if locked). Close with Back, X, or tap outside. Always-open: **How we score** (the honesty home, with About scoring), **Lodge Dinner**, **What skills are useful for**, plus Trivia teach graphs (Quantum Step, Thermo Steam, Snow Insulates, Dam Math, and more). Pattern Stick / Memory Twig / Speed Ember unlock after those Assess blocks. **Train Badge** after Train. **Warm Word** after Social Read.
8. **Train** — PRACTICE ONLY. Drills (Pattern / Memory / Speed warmups with feedback; about +20% time vs Assess, slower ramp) and Trivia (≥80 lodge science-comedy questions: Quantum · Math · Thermo · History · Lodge · Patterns · Camp life). After a Trivia answer, a **unique** Bober teach still for that question plus the punchline stay until **Next** (Next enables after 2.5s). XP and streaks save separately. Train never changes Assess bars or cohort %. Blur pauses Train. **Leave** (top-left) returns to the Train hub from a drill/trivia, or to Splash from the hub.
9. **Leave** on Assess (practice or scored) and Social Read asks once, then abandons the run without writing Profile bars. Museum card detail closes on tap outside the card (also Back and X).
10. **Social Read** — 10 lodge scenarios drawn from a pool of ≥40 (core Story 10 + extras). Soft 2–3 sentence feedback stays until **Next** (Next enables after 2.5s; no auto-advance). Practice index only — not an EQ score. Never feeds Reasoning / Memory / Speed.

Original camp tiles (sticks, dams, snow, lodge marks, beaver silhouettes, fish, embers, posts, tools). Skill facets inside Brain Camp — not a clinical or certified IQ test. Not a clinical plate.
