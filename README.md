# Bober Brain Camp

Fan game by a holder. Not affiliated with any token, studio, or official Bober project.

**Play online:** https://ccosma1.github.io/bober-brain-camp/

A phone-first camp of short puzzle rounds. Phase 1 is **Reasoning + Memory + Speed Assess**, a three-bar **Profile**, and a light **Museum**.

**Honesty:** Fan game skill index — not a clinical or diagnostic test. Not an IQ score. Results are skill bars (0–100) and beta cohort percentiles only. Bars are never averaged into one number. No clinical score, no official test.

Mission: Train at Brain Camp — puzzles, people sense, trivia. Not an official IQ score.

Scope: Short rounds. Play to improve, not to diagnose.

No wallet. No gacha. No PvP. No crypto connect.

## Local

Run `START.bat` or open `index.html` in a browser.

## How to play

1. **Splash** — art preloads (Bober stays visible). Pick **Assess Reasoning**, **Assess Memory**, or **Assess Speed**. **Train** opens drills + trivia (no separate splash Trivia button). **Social Read** is lodge-scenario practice (not an EQ score). **Museum** is open. **About scoring** opens the honesty popup. A camp in progress offers **Continue** / **Abandon**.
2. Each Assess starts with a short **Practice** (unscored), then **Start Assess**.
3. **Reasoning** — 3 practice + 12 matrix items (Form A or B). 50s each. Leaving the tab mid-item closes that item as missed. Retry flips Form A ↔ Form B.
4. **Memory** — 2 warmups + 10 trials. Study 1.8s per icon, 12s to tap. Starts at span 3; span rises after 3 correct at that span, drops after 2 wrong. A span-length banner (1.0s) shows before study. Reverse is labeled at item start and never flips mid-item. Blur during the response closes the item.
5. **Speed** — 5 warmups + 20 short trials. Four tiles: tap the odd one, or match the target. 3.2s each. Miss / timeout / leave-tab = missed.
6. **Profile** — Reasoning · Memory · Speed Assess bars, plus **Social Read** practice (lodge scenarios). Never one combined number. **Beta norms (early).** One **About scoring** popup (honesty + formulas). Social Read does not change Assess.
7. **Museum** — tap a card for detail (large art if unlocked, silhouette tease if locked). Close with Back, X, or tap outside. How we score (always). Pattern Stick / Memory Twig / Speed Ember unlock after those Assess blocks. **Train Badge** after Train. **Warm Word** after Social Read.
8. **Train** — PRACTICE ONLY. Drills (Pattern / Memory / Speed warmups with feedback; about +20% time vs Assess, slower ramp) and Trivia (≥80 lodge science-comedy questions: Quantum · Math · Thermo · History · Lodge · Patterns · Camp life). After a Trivia answer, the teach punchline stays until **Next** (Next enables after 2.5s). XP and streaks save separately. Train never changes Assess bars or cohort %. Blur pauses Train. **Leave** (top-left) returns to the Train hub from a drill/trivia, or to Splash from the hub.
9. **Leave** on Assess (practice or scored) and Social Read asks once, then abandons the run without writing Profile bars. Museum card detail closes on tap outside the card (also Back and X).
10. **Social Read** — 10 lodge scenarios drawn from a pool of ≥40 (core Story 10 + extras). Soft 2–3 sentence feedback stays until **Next** (Next enables after 2.5s; no auto-advance). Practice index only — not an EQ score. Never feeds Reasoning / Memory / Speed.

Original camp tiles (sticks, dams, snow, lodge marks, beaver silhouettes). Not a clinical plate.
