How scored

Each bar uses its own Assess only. Practice never counts. Bars are not averaged.

Reasoning: (correct ÷ 12) × 80, plus leftover time on correct items scaled to at most 20 then × (correct ÷ 12). Fast guessing cannot fill the bar.
